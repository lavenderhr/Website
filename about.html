Meet your HR consultant.

Experienced, practical HR support built around your organisation and the people who make it work.

Experience you can draw on

20+ years of HR leadership experience.

I am an MCIPD-qualified HR leader with over 20 years of experience working with senior management teams in both public and private sector multinational organisations, across unionised and non-unionised environments.

I take pride in getting to know you and your business to provide the most effective solutions. I offer a wealth of knowledge in talent management, coaching and mentoring, employee relations, workplace mediation, and creating robust policies and procedures.

Whether you are focused on building a positive workplace culture, resolving employee relations challenges, or navigating business change, I am here to help you achieve your goals with solutions tailored to your organisation's unique needs.
