What I can help with

Comprehensive HR solutions.

Flexible support from one-off advice to ongoing HR partnership and strategic projects.

Case Management

This support may include Absence Management, advice or investigations related to Disciplinary and Grievance matters, Performance Management, and workplace mediation.

Documentation

I can help create effective policies and procedures that align with your business goals and values. I can also review company handbooks and other HR materials.

Projects

Whether you're reviewing your workforce operating model, talent and succession plans, onboarding processes, or seeking ways to improve engagement and retention, I am here to help.

Ad Hoc Consultancy

Got a one-off issue that needs resolution? Need support without being locked into monthly payments? The pay-as-you-go option provides access to support for your specific issue or query, covering everything from recruitment to redundancy and everything in between.

Retained Support

If you're looking for regular HR support and advice, retained services may be exactly what you need. This flexible monthly package offers the benefits of in-house HR support without the hefty overheads, with direct access to the same consultant.

Tailored Solution / Project Arrangements

Each small business is unique, so I offer tailored solutions to meet your specific needs. I will take the time to understand you and your business, enabling me to create a proposal that aligns with your goals.
